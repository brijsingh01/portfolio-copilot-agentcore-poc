# Apex Wealth Management - Enterprise Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

## 1. Asset Allocation Limits by Risk Profile
- **Conservative**: Equities: 20% - 40% | Fixed Income: 50% - 70% | Cash: 5% - 15%
- **Moderate**: 
  - Maximum Equity Exposure: 65% of Total Portfolio Value.
  - Target Fixed Income / Hedging: Minimum 25%.
  - Cash Equivalents: Minimum 5%, Maximum 20%.
- **Aggressive**: Equities: 60% - 90% | Fixed Income: 10% - 30% | Cash: Minimum 2%.

## 2. Concentration & Single-Stock Limits
- **Single-Stock Ceiling**: No individual equity or corporate debt security may exceed 10% of total portfolio market value at the time of rebalancing.
- **Sector Concentration**: Exposure to any single GICS sector (e.g., Information Technology, Healthcare) must not exceed 30% of total equity allocation for Moderate profiles.
- **Remediation Trigger**: If organic market appreciation causes a single stock to exceed 12% of total portfolio value, the advisor must flag and plan rebalancing within 30 business days.

## 3. ESG Mandate & Negative Screening
For portfolios flagged with `esg_mandate: true`, investments are strictly prohibited in firms deriving more than 5% of gross revenue from:
- Controversial Weapons & Defense Systems
- Thermal Coal Extraction & Coal-Fired Power Generation
- Commercial Tobacco & Nicotine Products

## 4. Rebalancing & Execution Guidelines
- Capital deployments must calculate projected post-trade tax drag before execution.
- Idle cash exceeding 15% in Moderate accounts should be systematically deployed into diversified index funds or approved thematic targets that comply with concentration ceilings.
